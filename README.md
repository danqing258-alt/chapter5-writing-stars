# Chapter 5 Writing Stars — Spot the Excellent Answer!

A free, mobile-friendly GitHub Pages quiz for PUSL2048 Level 5 undergraduate research writing.

## How to play
Students compare a **basic** and **very good** example for each of four Chapter 5 sections, then select the stronger answer. The game explains why it better meets the marking criteria and awards a Writing Star. Wrong choices receive a hint and another attempt.

1. **5.1 Conclusion and Answers to Research Questions**
2. **5.2 Practical Business Recommendations**
3. **5.3 Research Limitations**
4. **5.4 Suggestions for Future Research**

The worked example is the university entrepreneurship study with ESE and PUS as predictors of participation intention (PI). The model examples use the teaching results: N=144, ESE–PI r≈0.410, PUS–PI r≈0.712, both p<0.001; separate regression R²≈0.1677 and 0.5070. These are **associations**, not causal or unique adjusted effects.

## Publish on GitHub Pages
1. Create a new **public** GitHub repository named `chapter5-writing-stars`.
2. Extract the ZIP and upload `index.html`, `style.css`, `app.js`, and `README.md` to the **root** of the repository.
3. In **Settings → Pages**, choose **Deploy from a branch**, `main`, `/ (root)`, then Save.
4. When deployment completes, visit `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/chapter5-writing-stars/`.
5. Test the four questions and the final award on a phone before sharing a QR code.

Progress is stored only in the student's browser, not sent to the instructor. Stars reward recognition of stronger writing, not formal assessment.
